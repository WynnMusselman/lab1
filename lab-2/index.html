Halloween History

Halloween has been celebrated for hundreds of years, especially in Ireland and Scotland. The holiday was brought over to the US by immigrants in the 1800s. Since becoming an American holiday, it is now celebrated with scary imager, carving pumpkins, and trick-or-treating!
